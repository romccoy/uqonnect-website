Safety

Community Guidelines

UQonnect is built for real communities. Treat the people behind the profiles accordingly.

Be respectful

Harassment, credible threats, targeted abuse, bullying and hateful conduct are not welcome on UQonnect.

Keep people safe

Do not encourage violence, exploitation, dangerous illegal activity or the sexual exploitation of any person. Content that creates a serious safety risk may be removed and, when appropriate, reported to relevant authorities.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate others, deliberately mislead users about your identity or professional status, manipulate engagement, scam users or coordinate fraudulent activity.

Respect privacy

Do not publish another person’s sensitive personal information without authorization or use UQonnect to stalk, intimidate or expose someone.

Respect ownership

Only upload content you have the right to use. UQonnect may respond to valid intellectual-property complaints as required by law.

Use reporting tools

If something appears to violate these Guidelines, report it through UQonnect’s in-app tools so it can be reviewed.
