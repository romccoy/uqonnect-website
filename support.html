UQonnect Support

Need help with your UQonnect account, safety, privacy, purchases, or app experience? We’re here to help.

Contact support

Email: support@uqonnect.com

Please include the email address or username associated with your UQonnect account and a brief description of the issue. Do not send your password.

Privacy and account requests

For privacy questions or account-related requests, contact support or review our Privacy Policy and Account Deletion page.

Safety reports

If you encounter content or behavior that violates UQonnect’s rules, use the reporting tools available in the app. For additional help, contact support.
